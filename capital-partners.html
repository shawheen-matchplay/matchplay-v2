Better companies. Better diligence. Better investor conversations.

MatchPlay Capital Partners connects investment-ready companies with investors whose stage, sector, check size, and strategy actually fit.

Companies enter the process prepared. Investors receive structured information. Both sides spend less time figuring out whether there is a fit and more time evaluating the opportunity.

Become a Capital Partner ↗ How It Works

Deal flow isn’t the problem. Finding the right signal is.

Investors see more companies than they can realistically evaluate.

Founders have the opposite problem: they often spend months approaching investors who were never a fit for the company in the first place.

MatchPlay Capital Partners sits between those two problems.

We combine:

01

Structured Diligence

Companies can enter investor conversations with a consistent diligence foundation.

02

Investor Intelligence

We understand capital partners by sector, stage, geography, check size, and investment strategy.

03

Better Matching

Companies are introduced based on fit rather than simply being distributed to a large investor list.

04

Transaction Infrastructure

When several investors want to participate, MatchPlay can help coordinate the SPV and administrative process behind the transaction.

How Capital Partners works.

  1. 01

    Companies Prepare

    Companies complete MatchPlay diligence and fundraising preparation before entering the broader Capital Partners process.

    The goal is simple: address the obvious questions before the first investor meeting.

    Companies organize:

    • Diligence findings
    • Pitch materials
    • Financials
    • Data room
    • Financing terms
    • Use of proceeds
    • Key milestones
    • Investor Q&A

  2. 02

    We Identify Fit

    MatchPlay evaluates which Capital Partners are relevant to the opportunity.

    This isn’t about putting a founder in front of every investor in the network. It’s about identifying the investors who should actually see the company.

    Matching can consider:

    • Sector
    • Stage
    • Check size
    • Geography
    • Investment thesis
    • Lead vs. co-invest preference
    • Strategic relevance
    • Portfolio construction
    • Follow-on capacity

  3. 03

    Investors Review

    Capital Partners receive organized information to help determine whether the opportunity deserves deeper review.

    Investors remain responsible for making their own investment decisions and conducting any additional diligence they consider appropriate.

    Depending on the company and process, materials may include:

    • Company overview
    • Pitch deck
    • MatchPlay diligence
    • Data-room access
    • Financing terms
    • Use of proceeds
    • Key risks
    • Management responses
    • Supporting documentation

  4. 04

    The Conversation Moves Forward

    MatchPlay helps keep the process organized as engagement progresses.

    MatchPlay provides the operational layer around the process while the company and investor determine whether there is an investment fit.

    1. Introduced
    2. Reviewing
    3. Meeting
    4. Diligence
    5. Interested
    6. Committed

A better starting point for diligence.

Every investor has its own process. MatchPlay isn’t trying to replace that.

We are trying to eliminate the unnecessary work that happens before serious diligence can even begin.

Instead of receiving:

  • Incomplete data rooms
  • Different versions of financials
  • Missing corporate documents
  • Unsupported market claims
  • Unanswered regulatory questions
  • Unclear financing requirements

Capital Partners can begin with a company that has already been through a structured preparation process.

Standardized where it helps. Flexible where it matters.

MatchPlay organizes diligence around a consistent framework so investors know where to find the information they need.

Typical areas include:

Company & Team

Leadership, organization, governance, and execution capability.

Product & Technology

Product maturity, differentiation, technical evidence, and development risks.

Market & Competition

Market need, positioning, customer validation, and competitive landscape.

Financial

Historical performance, projections, burn, runway, and financing requirements.

IP

Ownership, patents, licensing, and defensibility.

Regulatory & Reimbursement

Pathway, strategy, market access, and reimbursement where relevant.

Financing

Round terms, valuation context, use of proceeds, and milestone planning.

Capital Partners is built around fit—not volume.

A large investor database isn’t particularly useful if nobody knows who actually invests in what.

MatchPlay is building a deeper understanding of the investors in the network.

We want to know:

What do you invest in?

Sector and subsector preferences.

When do you invest?

Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A, or later stages.

How much do you invest?

Typical initial and follow-on check sizes.

How do you participate?

Lead, co-lead, co-investor, or syndicate participant.

What matters to you?

Geography, technology, business model, regulatory profile, commercial traction, and strategic fit.

The more we understand your strategy…

…the more relevant the opportunities we can put in front of you.

What Capital Partners is—and isn’t.

Capital Partners is:

  • A relationship network connecting prepared companies with investors whose investment strategies align.
  • A source of structured company information and diligence.
  • A way for investors to refer companies for deeper evaluation.
  • A connection point for SPV and syndication infrastructure.

Capital Partners is not:

  • A guarantee that a company will receive financing.
  • A requirement that an investor participate in any opportunity.
  • A substitute for an investor’s own investment decision or diligence.
  • A MatchPlay Ventures investment recommendation.

This distinction matters.
