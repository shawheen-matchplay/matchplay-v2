How it works.

Five steps from sourcing to your decision. Only the last one is yours, and it’s always optional.

01

We source

Broadly: founders, other funds, universities, accelerators, industry relationships, and MatchPlay Group.

02

We diligence

Team, market, technology, IP, regulatory, commercialization, financials, and valuation. Depth scales with sector, stage, and complexity.

03

We decide

Diligence informs the decision; it doesn’t make it. MatchPlay Ventures decides independently, and being a MatchPlay Group client never guarantees its capital.

04

We invest

When we have conviction, we invest directly or sponsor an SPV, then invite Capital Partners to review the opportunity.

05

You decide

Participation is optional, deal by deal, based on your own thesis, risk tolerance, and check size.

One vehicle. Clear economics.

Most opportunities are structured as a single MatchPlay-sponsored SPV, with every cost disclosed before you commit.

Invest through an SPV

Capital Partners invest together through one vehicle: one subscription and one position on the company’s cap table.

  • Centralized subscriptions and reporting
  • K-1 administration
  • Follow-on coordination
  • Distribution management

Administrative, banking, tax, and compliance functions may be handled by specialized third-party providers.

Economics disclosed up front

Depending on the deal, economics may include:

SPV administration
Costs to establish and maintain the vehicle.
Management fee
Applies to certain MatchPlay-sponsored investments.
Carried interest
A share of profits to MatchPlay Ventures, only after invested capital is returned.
Third-party expenses
Legal, filing, tax, and banking costs.

Terms vary by transaction and are disclosed before subscription.

After the wire clears.

The relationship continues through the life of the investment.

Company updates

Operating, financing, commercial, regulatory, and clinical milestones.

Portfolio reporting

Investment documents and SPV information in one place.

Investor events

Founder sessions and portfolio updates.

Distributions

Exit proceeds coordinated through your investment vehicle.

Follow-ons

Invest again, with context.
