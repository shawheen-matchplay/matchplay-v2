What we evaluate

Team
Experience, gaps, governance, and execution.
Market
Size, customer need, adoption, and competition.
Product & Technology
Differentiation, validation, development stage, defensibility.
Financials
Burn, runway, projections, and unit economics.
Intellectual Property
Ownership, patents, licensing, freedom to operate.
Regulatory & Reimbursement
Regulatory pathway, reimbursement, and market access.
Financing
Round size, valuation, and use of proceeds.

What you walk away with

  • Investor materials: pitch, summary, financing narrative, and Q&A.
  • A data room organized the way investors run diligence.
  • A financing strategy: raise size, milestones, valuation, and dilution.
  • Founder prep for the questions that come after the first pitch.
