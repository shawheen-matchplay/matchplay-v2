Vision

The world is innovating.
But who’s got the wheel?

We believe the future should be shaped by the best ideas and founders — not simply the best-connected, or those in a West Coast zip code.

Problem

Capital has more deal flow than ever, but not enough signal.

Too often, capital follows capital, while great founders outside the usual networks go unseen.

Mission

Find the consistent. Create the signal. Back the future.

MatchPlay eliminates friction between founders and capital, pressure-tests founders for clarity, conviction, and execution, and creates the signal investors need to act with conviction.

MatchPlay Ventures invests, while MatchPlay Capital Partners — our 160+ active investor syndicate — provides a broader network of capital and conviction.
