Fundraising starts before the first investor meeting.

The strongest fundraising processes don’t start with a list of investors. They start with understanding whether the company, financing, materials, and diligence can stand up to investor scrutiny.

MatchPlay brings those pieces together in one process.

  1. 01

    Diligence

    Understand what investors will see.

    Evaluate the company across the areas that drive an investment decision—from team, market, product and financials to IP, regulatory strategy, reimbursement, competition, and financing.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Address the gaps before outreach begins.

    Turn diligence findings into stronger investor materials, a more complete data room, clearer financing milestones, and better answers to the questions investors are likely to ask.

  3. 03

    Fundraise

    Focus on investors that actually fit.

    Capital Partners helps qualified companies identify and engage investors based on stage, sector, check size, geography, and investment strategy.

  4. 04

    Syndicate

    Bring multiple investors together through one vehicle.

    When several investors want to participate, MatchPlay can coordinate an SPV that simplifies closing and ongoing investor administration.

Start with diligence. Know what investors will ask before they ask it.

Most founders don’t get meaningful feedback when an investor passes. MatchPlay gives founders a structured view of the company from an investor’s perspective before those issues become reasons to say no.

Our diligence process evaluates:

Team

Experience, organizational gaps, governance, and execution capability.

Market

Market size, customer need, adoption, and competitive dynamics.

Product & Technology

Differentiation, technical validation, development status, and defensibility.

Financials

Burn, runway, projections, unit economics, and capital requirements.

Intellectual Property

Ownership, patent position, licensing, and freedom-to-operate considerations.

Regulatory & Reimbursement

Commercial pathways, regulatory strategy, reimbursement, and market-access considerations where applicable.

Financing

Round size, valuation, use of proceeds, milestones, and future capital requirements.

The result isn’t just a report.

It becomes the foundation for your fundraising process.

Bring investors together without creating cap-table chaos.

When several investors want to participate in the same financing, MatchPlay can coordinate a single investment vehicle that consolidates their participation.

For Companies

Bring multiple investors into one SPV rather than adding numerous individual investors to the cap table.

For Syndicate Organizers

Use MatchPlay’s infrastructure to coordinate investor participation, closing, and ongoing vehicle administration.

Capabilities can include:

  • SPV formation coordination
  • Investor onboarding
  • Digital subscriptions
  • Accredited investor verification
  • KYC/AML workflow
  • Commitment tracking
  • Funding coordination
  • Reporting
  • K-1 administration
  • Follow-on participation
  • Transfer coordination

Specialized third-party providers support legal, regulatory, tax, banking, and administrative functions where appropriate.

Simple, upfront SPV pricing.

Each SPV is subject to setup and administration fees based on the vehicle structure, investor count, and required ongoing support.

Fees are agreed before launch and are not calculated as a percentage of the capital raised.

Third-party legal, filing, banking, tax, and other external costs are disclosed separately where applicable.
