For Investors

You don’t have a deal flow problem.
You have a signal problem.

Every investor sees more companies than they can evaluate. Almost none have a reliable way to tell which ones are real. MatchPlay standardises the diligence and the data room, so what reaches you is comparable, verified, and ready to act on.

Join the community ↗ See the offering

The problem

The current signal
is that someone rich said yes.

Most allocation still follows a lead. But leads are often not close to the product or the industry, and frequently have not run real diligence themselves. Following that signal is following a guess. Y Combinator standardised the pitch deck. We are doing it for diligence and the data room.

Our approach.

01

Standardised diligence

Every company runs the same institutional-quality process, so two companies can actually be compared rather than pitched.

02

Data rooms that match

One structure, the same sections in the same order. You stop re-learning where the numbers live for every deal.

03

Decide faster

Findings are written down and shared, not rediscovered. First meeting to close gets materially shorter.

How participation works.

The vehicle is formed and administered by MatchPlay Group before a deal reaches the syndicate — so you are joining a structure that already exists rather than assembling one.

  1. 01

    Diligence

    The company completes a standardised diligence and data room.

  2. 02

    Review

    MatchPlay Ventures independently decides what warrants investment.

  3. 03

    Syndicate

    The deal is syndicated into a single administered vehicle.

  4. 04

    Hold

    The same structure carries reporting, follow-ons, and transfers.

The investor offering.

MatchPlay Group

Diligence and structure.

Runs the diligence, holds the data room, and sets up and administers the investment vehicle — onboarding, signatures, fund flows, and post-closing administration. Syndication happens here.

MatchPlay Ventures

Direct investment.

The investing arm. Reviews companies that come through the ecosystem, decides independently what is investable, and puts its own capital in alongside the syndicate.
